## Figueroa et al. (2024): ZFHX3 repeat expansion and SCA4

**A GGC-repeat expansion in ZFHX3 encoding polyglycine causes spinocerebellar ataxia type 4 and impairs autophagy.** Nature Genetics 56, 1080–1089. [Publisher article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-024-01719-5); DOI: 10.1038/s41588-024-01719-5.

This study provides a useful example of comparing local chromosome patterns. Its family/founder investigation differs from our planned comparison of haplotype frequencies among Diabetes, Prediabetes, and Control participants. Our completed cohort run currently has no usable haplotype panels.
